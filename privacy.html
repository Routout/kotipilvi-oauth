Privacy policy – kotipilvi-peili

kotipilvi-peili is used only by its owner, for the owner's own Google account.

  • Data accessed: Google Drive files and Gmail messages, with read-only scopes (drive.readonly, gmail.readonly).
  • Use: the data is copied to the owner's own home server as a personal backup. Nothing is written to or deleted from the Google account.
  • Sharing: the data is not shared with, sold to or transferred to any third party, and it is not used for advertising or for training AI models.
  • Storage: the copy stays on hardware the owner controls. Access tokens are stored only on that server.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
